About Nora

Ancient wisdom,
transformational touch

Welcome! I am Hannah, the founder of Nora Holistic Wellbeing. I am passionate about making women feel their best. I don't just provide regular treatments - I provide full self-care rituals.

The moment you step into the cosy Ladywell home clinic you will be greeted by the smell of warm tea and incense, surrounded by plenty of plants, and organic cotton and linen sheets that are always freshly washed.

I offer somatic treatments for women only, spanning womb massage, lymphatic drainage massage and holistic facials. Each treatment draws on a different tradition — Mizan therapy, Ayurveda, TCM, Kobido — but they share the same intention: helping the body remember how to heal.
